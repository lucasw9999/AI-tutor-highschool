# Day-1 Diagnostic — Digital SAT Placement (~40 min, for Lucas)

Not a test you can fail — it places you so the tutor targets your real weak spots from session one. Two short parts (RW + Math). Do it in your tutor (paste your answers) or on paper, then tell the tutor how it went. **For every question, also note *why* you picked your answer** — that's what reveals the fix.

> Best version: take an official **Bluebook** full practice test when you have ~2¼ hours — it's adaptive and gives a real score. This paper diagnostic is the quick stand-in.

---

## Part A — Reading & Writing (12 Qs of mixed type)
*(The tutor will generate these live in SAT format; sample shapes below so you know what's coming.)*
- **Grammar (punctuation):** "Choose the answer that conforms to Standard English conventions" — comma vs semicolon vs colon vs dash; sentence boundaries.
- **Agreement/verbs/modifiers/parallelism.**
- **Central idea / main purpose** of a short passage.
- **Command of evidence** — textual ("which quote best supports…") and **quantitative** (read a graph/table to support a claim).
- **Words-in-context** — best word for the blank.
- **Transitions** — best logical connector.
- **Rhetorical synthesis** — given bullet notes + a goal, pick the choice that meets the goal.

## Part B — Math (10 Qs, mixed; Desmos allowed)
- **Algebra:** solve a linear system; interpret slope/intercept in context.
- **Advanced Math:** solve a quadratic; evaluate/transform a function; an exponential-growth setup.
- **Problem-Solving & Data Analysis:** a percent/ratio word problem; read a scatterplot / two-way table; mean vs median.
- **Geometry & Trig:** a triangle (special-right or Pythagorean); a circle (area/arc); one right-triangle trig (SOH-CAH-TOA).
- Include **1 grid-in** (no answer choices) to practice the format.

---

## How your answers set your plan
- **Grammar slips** → start at `rw-grammar` (fastest points on the whole test).
- **Reading misses** → note the *type* (evidence? inference? vocab?) → `rw-reading` targets it.
- **Math:** since you've done Algebra 2, misses are usually **careless / misread / forgot a Desmos shortcut**, not "can't do it" — the tutor logs which, and that becomes your error log.
- **Pacing:** note any section you couldn't finish in time.

**Then:** tell the tutor *"diagnostic done"* + your answers and the *why* for each. It sets your levels in `mastery-tracker.md`, names your 2 weakest areas, and starts you on the highest-ROI one. After that: **"let's continue"** each session.
