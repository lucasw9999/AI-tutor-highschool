# The Error Log — the #1 lever to 1500+

**Why this matters for your score:** near the top, you don't gain points by learning *new* content — you gain them by **never missing the same kind of question twice**. The error log is the tool that makes that happen. Top scorers swear by it. If you do one thing besides practice, do this.

---

## The idea
Every question you miss **or** get right but felt unsure about goes in the log. You tag *why* you missed it, write the fix, and **re-do it later** until it's automatic. Over a few weeks your repeated mistakes disappear — and that's exactly the gap between ~1300 and 1500+.

## The four reasons you miss a question (tag every miss)
Diagnosing *why* matters more than the question itself, because each has a different fix:
| Tag | What it means | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| **CONTENT** | You didn't know the rule/concept | Learn it (study pack) + drill that type |
| **CARELESS** | You knew it but slipped (sign error, misbubbled, skipped a step) | Process fix: check the step you skipped; slow down 5 sec |
| **MISREAD** | You misunderstood the question/passage (answered the wrong thing, fell for a trap) | Read the question stem twice; underline what it actually asks |
| **TIMING** | You rushed/guessed because you were behind | Pacing drill; flag-and-return strategy |

> **Careless + misread together usually outnumber content gaps near the top** — and they're the *fastest* points to recover. Don't dismiss them as "silly mistakes"; they're your biggest score reservoir.

## The template (copy this; one row per miss)
```
| Date | Section | Question type/domain | My answer | Right answer | WHY (content/careless/misread/timing) | The rule/fix in one line | Re-do date | Re-do result |
```

**Example rows:**
```
| 6/10 | RW | comma splice            | B | A | content  | two complete clauses need ;/period/FANBOYS, not a comma | 6/13 | ✓ |
| 6/10 | Math | quadratic (vertex)     | C | D | careless | dropped the negative when completing the square          | 6/13 | ✓ |
| 6/10 | RW | command of evidence     | A | C | misread  | answer must support THE CLAIM, not just be true          | 6/14 | ✗→re-do |
| 6/10 | Math | data — mean vs median  | — | B | timing   | guessed; ran out of time in Module 2                     | 6/14 | ✓ |
```

## The weekly routine (15 min)
1. **After every practice set/test:** log every miss + every "lucky" right answer.
2. **Re-do** each logged question 2–3 days later (cover your old work). Mark ✓ or ✗.
3. **Weekly review:** sort by the WHY tag. Your biggest tag tells you this week's focus:
   - Lots of **CONTENT** → hit that study pack.
   - Lots of **CARELESS** → build a personal "checklist before I submit" (e.g., "did I answer what it asked? sign right? units?").
   - Lots of **MISREAD** → slow down on the question stem; practice predicting the answer before reading choices.
   - Lots of **TIMING** → do timed sections; practice flag-and-return.
4. A question leaves the log only after you've gotten it right **on a re-do** (✓).

## What it looks like when it's working
- Your repeated mistakes vanish (you stop re-missing the same comma rule / sign error / trap).
- Your "careless" tag count drops session over session — that's your score going up.
- By test day, the log is short and the patterns are gone.

*Bring your top 3 recurring error-patterns into your head on test day as a personal checklist. That alone can be worth 30–50 points.*
