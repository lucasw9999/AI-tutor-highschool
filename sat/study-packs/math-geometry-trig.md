# Math — Geometry & Trigonometry (Digital SAT)

**Why this matters for your score:** ~**15% of Math**. The SAT **gives you a reference sheet** with most area/volume formulas — so the points come from *setting up* the right relationship (similar triangles, the Pythagorean theorem, circle facts, SOH-CAH-TOA), not memorizing formulas. Know what's on the sheet vs what you must memorize, and these are reliable points.

> **On the provided reference sheet** (don't memorize these): area & circumference of a circle, area of triangle/rectangle, the Pythagorean theorem, **special right triangles (30-60-90 and 45-45-90)**, volumes (box, cylinder, sphere, cone, pyramid), and the facts "360° / 2π radians in a circle," "180° in a triangle." **Must memorize** (NOT on the sheet): SOH-CAH-TOA, the circle equation, arc/sector proportions, similar-triangle ratios, parallel-line angle rules.

---

## The must-know concepts

### 1. Lines & angles
Straight line = 180°; angles around a point = 360°; **vertical angles equal**; with **parallel lines + a transversal**, corresponding/alternate angles are equal and same-side angles are supplementary (add to 180°).
**#1 trap:** assuming lines are parallel/perpendicular unless told (or marked).

### 2. Triangles
- Angles sum to **180°**. Exterior angle = sum of the two remote interior angles.
- **Pythagorean** `a² + b² = c²` (right triangles). Common triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
- **Special right triangles (on the sheet):** 45-45-90 → sides `x, x, x√2`; 30-60-90 → sides `x, x√3, 2x`.
- **Similar triangles:** equal angles → **proportional sides**. Set up a ratio.
**Worked example:** A 6-ft person casts a 4-ft shadow; a tree casts a 20-ft shadow at the same time. Tree height? Similar triangles: 6/4 = h/20 → h = **30 ft**.
**#1 trap:** forgetting similar-triangle side ratios are **constant**; mixing up which sides correspond.

### 3. Circles
- **Area** = πr², **circumference** = 2πr (on the sheet).
- **Arc length** = (θ/360)·2πr; **sector area** = (θ/360)·πr² (θ in degrees). In radians: arc = rθ, sector = ½r²θ.
- **Equation of a circle:** `(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²`, center (h, k), radius r. (Must memorize — completing the square may be needed.)
**Worked example:** Circle `(x−2)² + (y+3)² = 25` → center **(2, −3)**, radius **5**.
**#1 trap:** sign of the center (it's the *opposite* of what's in the parentheses); using diameter for r.

### 4. Area & volume
On the sheet — just plug in. Watch **units** (cm² vs cm³) and read whether they want surface area or volume.

### 5. Right-triangle trig (SOH-CAH-TOA) — memorize this
- **sin = Opp/Hyp, cos = Adj/Hyp, tan = Opp/Adj.**
- **Co-function:** sin(x°) = cos(90° − x°) — the SAT tests this directly.
**Worked example:** In a right triangle, the side opposite angle A is 3 and the hypotenuse is 5. sin A = **3/5**; cos A = 4/5 (other leg = 4).
**#1 trap:** picking the wrong side relative to the angle; forgetting which side is the hypotenuse (always opposite the right angle).

### 6. Radians (basics in scope)
**180° = π radians.** Convert: degrees·(π/180) ↔ radians·(180/π). Arc length = rθ when θ is in radians.
**Worked example:** 60° = 60·π/180 = **π/3** radians.

## Graduated practice (try, then check) — [GI] = grid-in
**P1.** A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. Find the hypotenuse. `[GI]`
**P2.** In a 30-60-90 triangle the shortest side is 5. Find the hypotenuse and the longer leg.
**P3.** A circle has area 36π. What is its circumference?
**P4.** Circle: `x² + y² − 6x + 4y = 12`. Find the center and radius. (Complete the square.)
**P5.** Two similar triangles: small has sides 3-4-5; the large one's longest side is 15. Find its perimeter.
**P6.** A sector has central angle 90° in a circle of radius 8. Find the sector's area.
**P7.** Right triangle, angle θ with cos θ = 8/17. Find sin θ.
**P8.** Convert 135° to radians.

### Answers
- **P1:** √(81+144) = √225 = **15**.
- **P2:** hyp = 2·5 = **10**; longer leg = 5√3 ≈ **8.66**.
- **P3:** πr² = 36π → r = 6 → circumference = 2π·6 = **12π**.
- **P4:** group: (x²−6x) + (y²+4y) = 12 → (x−3)²−9 + (y+2)²−4 = 12 → (x−3)²+(y+2)² = 25 → center **(3, −2)**, r = **5**.
- **P5:** scale factor = 15/5 = 3 → perimeter = 3·(3+4+5) = 3·12 = **36**.
- **P6:** (90/360)·π·8² = ¼·64π = **16π**.
- **P7:** other leg = √(17²−8²)=√225=15 → sin θ = 15/17 (opp/hyp). **15/17**.
- **P8:** 135·π/180 = **3π/4**.

## Don't get these wrong
1. **Use the reference sheet** — don't waste memory on area/volume; do memorize SOH-CAH-TOA, the circle equation, arc/sector ratios.
2. **Circle center sign** flips from the parentheses; complete the square when the equation is expanded.
3. **Similar triangles** = constant side ratio → scale perimeters/sides by the factor.
4. **Special-right ratios** (x√2, x√3) save time — recognize them.
5. **Units**: area vs volume, and degrees vs radians for arc/sector.
