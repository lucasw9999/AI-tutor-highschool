# How To Use This — Plain Instructions (for Lucas)

Your SAT catch-up. Goal: **1500+** at the **Saturday 6 March 2027 sitting** — fast, dense, and **by doing questions**, not reading textbooks. You're busy, so this is built to take the *shortest* time for the *most* points.

> **⏰ The one date that matters: registration for 6 March 2027 closes Friday 19 February 2027.** Miss it and the next clean sitting is a year of waiting, because the May 2027 SAT lands 10 days before your AP exams. Full table in `README.md`.

> **🎓 Sooner than that: the PSAT, sometime in 1–30 October 2026.** Your **school** signs you up for that one — there's nothing for you to register for, which is why people miss it. As a sophomore it's your **free official dress rehearsal** for March: real room, real timing, real proctor. Ask your counselor in the first week of school whether you're on the roster, which date, and whether it's the PSAT/NMSQT or PSAT 10. (The National Merit one is widely understood to be *next* October, junior year — `README.md` spells out what's verified and what isn't.)

## The big idea
The SAT is a **finite set of repeating question types** + not making mistakes. So you don't "study" it — you **drill question types** until they're automatic, learn the one rule/trick on each miss, and keep an **error log** so you never miss the same thing twice. That error log is the single biggest reason people break 1500.

## What you actually do
- **One-time:** set up the tutor (`chatgpt-setup.md`, ~5 min).
- **Each session (~30–40 min):** the tutor fires SAT-style questions, you answer + say *why*, it coaches the miss and logs the error. End → paste the `TRACKER UPDATE` into `mastery-tracker.md`.
- **Weekly:** review your error log; every ~2–3 weeks take a **full adaptive practice test in Bluebook** (the real thing) and feed the results back.

## The order (express plan)
1. **Diagnostic** → find your 2 weakest areas.
2. **`rw-grammar`** (fastest points anywhere on the test) + the **Math** pack for your weakest domain.
3. **`rw-reading`** + **`rw-expression`** (method-driven).
4. **`desmos-calculator-skills`** + **`test-strategy-and-pacing`**.
5. **Full Bluebook practice tests** + relentless **error log** → repeat to 1500+.

## A session looks like this
1. Open the tutor, type **"let's continue"** (first time: **"let's start"** → diagnostic).
2. It gives you a question. Answer it **and say why** you chose it.
3. Wrong (or right-for-the-wrong-reason)? It tells you *which kind* of mistake (content / careless / misread / timing), coaches the fix, logs it.
4. It ratchets difficulty up as you get a type right (just like the adaptive test).
5. ~35 min in → `TRACKER UPDATE` → paste into `mastery-tracker.md`.

## Two non-negotiables for 1500+
- **Keep the error log** (`reference/error-log.md`) and **re-do** logged misses until automatic.
- **Module-1 accuracy:** the test is adaptive — being accurate early unlocks the higher-scoring second module. Slow down enough to be right early.

## If it drifts
- Too much explaining → "just give me questions."
- Too easy → "go harder / Module-2 level."
- Want a timed feel → "drill me at pace."
