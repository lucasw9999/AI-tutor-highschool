# Digital SAT Coverage Map — Express, Question-Driven (Lucas)

**Goal:** **1500+** on the Digital SAT, ready by **spring 2027** (and ideally earlier). This is the dense, question-driven catch-up — cover *every* question type, but spend reps where his answers reveal weakness. *Spec verified against College Board (satsuite.collegeboard.org).*

## The test (what a 1500+ is made of)
| Section | Questions | Time | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reading & Writing (RW)** | 54 | 64 min (two 32-min modules) | each Q has its own short passage |
| **Math** | 44 | 70 min (two 35-min modules) | ~75% multiple-choice, ~25% grid-in; **built-in Desmos** allowed throughout |
| **Total** | **98** | **~2h14m** (10-min break) | scored **400–1600** (RW 200–800 + Math 200–800) |

**It's multistage ADAPTIVE:** each section's **Module 1** sets whether **Module 2** is harder or easier — *and your Module-1 performance sets your score ceiling.* **→ Accuracy early matters most.**

## Where a 1500+ is actually won
1. **Accuracy, not new content.** Lucas has the math (post-Algebra 2); the SAT is a *finite set of question types* + not making mistakes.
2. **The error log.** Logging every miss (content gap vs careless vs misread vs timing) and re-doing it is the single biggest lever near the top. Non-negotiable.
3. **Grammar first (RW).** Standard English Conventions is a small set of rules → fastest points on the test.
4. **Module 1 accuracy.** Adaptive means early accuracy unlocks the high-scoring Module 2.

## Express crunch order
1. **Diagnostic** → find his weak domains.
2. **`rw-grammar`** (fastest RW points) + the **Math** pack for his weakest domain.
3. **`rw-reading`** + **`rw-expression`** (technique-heavy).
4. **`desmos-calculator-skills`** + **`test-strategy-and-pacing`**.
5. **Full adaptive practice tests** (Bluebook) + relentless **error log** → repeat to 1500+.
