# How To Use This — Plain Instructions (for Lucas)

This is your English project. The goal: walk into sophomore English already understanding it, and earn an **A** — mostly by getting good at the **analytical essay** (that's where the grade is won) and staying on top of vocab, reading, and exams.

## The big idea
You don't slowly read every page of every book and re-read the textbook. You work through **study packs** (one per book) and **skill guides** by answering questions with an AI tutor. When you miss something, the tutor explains just that piece. Learning happens by **doing + getting corrected** — fast and not boring.

## What you actually do
- **One-time:** set up the tutor (see `chatgpt-setup.md`, ~5 min).
- **Each session (~30–40 min):** the tutor quizzes you, walks you through analysis, and coaches your writing. At the end it prints a `TRACKER UPDATE` — paste it into `mastery-tracker.md`.
- **Keep two free-point habits airtight:** weekly **vocab** and turning in **homework/study-guide questions**. Never zero those.

## The order to go through it (a few-weeks plan)
1. **`guides/essay-writing.md`** first — this single skill lifts every essay grade. Do the drills.
2. The **study pack** for the book you're about to do in class (or **Julius Caesar** to start the year).
3. **`guides/vocabulary.md`** — learn the method, start the weekly habit.
4. **`guides/literary-devices.md`** — skim all, deep-dive the ones your current book uses.
5. **`guides/grammar.md`** and **`guides/research-mla.md`** as those assessments come up.

## A session looks like this
1. Open your tutor, type **"let's continue"** (first time: **"let's start"** → it runs a short diagnostic).
2. It asks you one question or gives one small task. You answer in the chat.
3. Stuck? Type **"hint."** It nudges, you try again.
4. For writing, you draft a thesis / paragraph; it grades you against Mrs. P's rubric and tells you exactly what to fix.
5. ~35 min in, it prints a `TRACKER UPDATE` → paste into `mastery-tracker.md`.

## Important (your integrity + your grade)
Mrs. P bans AI-written work and checks turnitin.com. **The tutor coaches and quizzes you — it does not write your essays.** Use it to get *good*, then write your own work. That's the whole point, and it's how the A is real.

## If it drifts
- Too much explaining → "just ask me questions."
- Too easy → "go deeper / harder."
- Confused → "give me a hint first."
