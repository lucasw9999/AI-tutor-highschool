Not a test you can fail — it places you so the tutor never wastes your time. It checks (A) a few **Algebra-2 foundations** precalc leans on, then (B) **one probe per unit** to see where you already stand. Work it in your tutor (paste it in) or on paper, then tell the tutor how it went. **Show your work.**

## Part A — Algebra-2 foundations (quick — these power everything)
**A1.** Factor completely: `2x² − 5x − 3`.
**A2.** Simplify: `(x³·x⁵) / x²` and `(2x²y)³`.
**A3.** Solve for x: `3(x − 4) = 2x + 5`.
**A4.** Evaluate without a calculator: `2⁻³` and `8^(2/3)`.
**A5.** What's the slope and y-intercept of `2x + 3y = 12`?

## Part B — One probe per unit
**B1 (Unit 1 — Polynomial/Rational):** For `f(x) = (x−1)/(x²−4)`, name the vertical asymptotes and the horizontal asymptote.
**B2 (Unit 2 — Exp/Log):** Solve `2ˣ = 16`, and rewrite `log₂(8) = 3` in exponential form.
**B3 (Unit 3 — Trig):** Without a calculator, give `sin(30°)`, `cos(π/2)`, and explain what radians are.
**B4 (Unit 3 — Sinusoidal):** For `y = 3sin(x) + 1`, what are the amplitude and the midline?
**B5 (Unit 4 — class only):** Do you know what a "vector" or a "matrix" is, even loosely? (Yes/no is fine.)
