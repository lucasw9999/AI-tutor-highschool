## The exam (what a 4–5 is made of) — **Tue, May 11, 2027** (Session 1)

> ⚠️ **AP CSA is the very next day — Wed, May 12, 2027.** Two AP exams back to back; plan the final week around both.
> ⚠️ **The exam format changed for May 2027** (Lucas's year). Numbers below are the new format (College Board CED Clarifications, effective Fall 2026). **Course content is unchanged** — all study packs stay valid. Details + source: `reference/2027-exam-changes.md`.

| Section | Count | Weight | Calculator |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I. Multiple Choice** | **42 Q** | **~63%** | Part A: **29 Q NO calc · 65 min** (~44%) · Part B: **13 Q calc · 40 min** |
| **II. Free Response** | 4 Q | **~37%** | Part A: Q1–Q2 **calc · 35 min** · Part B: Q3–Q4 **NO calc · 35 min** |

**The 4 FRQs are fixed types every year:**
1. **Function Concepts** (calculator) — *minor Part C(i) tweak for 2027*
2. **Modeling a Non-Periodic Context** (calculator) — *restructured for 2027: Part B(iii) removed, Part C expanded*
3. **Modeling a Periodic Context** (no calculator)
4. **Symbolic Manipulation** (no calculator) — **lowest-scoring FRQ both years (mean 1.28/6 in 2024, 1.93/6 in 2025); biggest point-gain opportunity**

Hybrid digital: MCQ in Bluebook; FRQs handwritten. **Calculator: an approved physical graphing calculator is permitted, *and* a built-in Desmos is available in Bluebook** → see `reference/graphing-calculator-skills.md`.

**Encouraging:** ~**50% (2024) → ~54% (2025) → 58% (2026) of all students score a 4 or 5** — the 2026 breakdown was 5 = 29%, 4 = 29%, 3 = 24%, 2 = 11%, 1 = 7%, so **82% scored 3 or higher** (verified 2026-08-11). A strong score is genuinely achievable with focused prep.

> **Key fact:** the AP exam tests **Units 1–3 only.** Unit 4 is required for the *class* but never appears on the *exam* — so we learn it for the A, but don't drill exam-format questions on it.

## The 4 units (your `study-packs/`)
| Unit | Topic | MCQ weight | Exam? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Polynomial & Rational Functions** — rates of change/concavity, polynomial end behavior & zeros, rational asymptotes/holes, transformations, function models | **30–40%** 🔥 | ✅ |
| **2** | **Exponential & Logarithmic** — sequences (arith/geo), exp growth/decay, composition, inverses, logs & log rules, solving exp/log equations, modeling, semi-log plots | **27–40%** 🔥 | ✅ |
| **3** | **Trigonometric & Polar** — unit circle/radians, sinusoidal functions (amp/period/midline/phase), tangent, inverse trig, trig equations, sec/csc/cot, identities, polar | **30–35%** 🔥 | ✅ |
| **4** | **Parameters, Vectors & Matrices** — parametric & implicit functions, conics, vectors, matrices, linear transformations | **not tested** | class only |

All three exam units carry roughly equal weight — **none is skippable.**

## Where the class grade is won
- **Tests/quizzes** (the bulk) — the unit packs' practice sets target these.
- **The FRQ-style free-response** — distinct skill (showing work + notation); the exam-strategy guide + Units 1–3 packs drill it.
- **Homework** — free points; never zero them.

## What "ready" means
Per unit: can work the practice set independently (calc *and* no-calc), knows the must-memorize formulas cold, and can do the relevant FRQ type. Track it in `mastery-tracker.md`.

## Express crunch order (a few weeks)
1. **`reference/formula-and-identity-sheet.md`** — memorize the unit circle + log/trig rules first; everything leans on them.
2. **Unit 1**, then **Unit 2**, then **Unit 3** (do these in order — each builds on the last). Highest exam yield.
3. **`reference/graphing-calculator-skills.md`** + **exam-strategy** once you're working FRQs.
4. **Unit 4** last (class-only; lightest).
